EID-978 is a nondescript red box. It has no markings of any kind on any side. Initially it appears to be made out of cardboard, but on closer inspection has a metallic surface that's always cold to the touch

It has appeared in the following locations:

- The Northern Oregon forest
- Locker 67 at ██████ stadium
- The janitor closet of EID-SITE-████
